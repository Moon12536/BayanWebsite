Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Bayan Premium.

Can I change my Premium plan?

Yes. Your Premium status is managed per Discord server. The available upgrade options depend on the plan system configured by Bayan.

Does Premium apply to my account?

Premium is attached to the selected Discord server rather than your personal Discord account.

Can I manage multiple servers?

Yes. After logging in with Discord, you can select a server that your Discord account has permission to manage.

Is Lifetime really lifetime?

The Lifetime plan is designed to have no automatic expiration.

Can the owner grant Premium?

The Bayan owner dashboard can grant or remove Premium for servers without requiring the server owner to purchase a plan.
